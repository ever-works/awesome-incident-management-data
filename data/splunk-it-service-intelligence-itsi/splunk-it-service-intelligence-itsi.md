## Overview

Splunk IT Service Intelligence (ITSI) is a unified security and observability solution powered by AI, designed to help organizations prevent major issues, ensure quick recovery, and maintain resilience.

## Features

- Real Time Alerting
- Anomaly Detection
- Threshold Alerts
- Custom Alert Actions
- Webhook Integration
- Alert Acknowledgement
- Email Alerts
- Incident Management

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises
