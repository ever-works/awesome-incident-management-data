## Overview

NotiLens is a real-time business monitoring tool that alerts teams when critical events fail, spike, anomaly, or go unexpectedly silent. Built for founders and dev teams running Stripe, servers, cron jobs, automation and AI agents.

## Features

- Real-time business monitoring
- Critical event failure alerts
- Spike detection
- Anomaly detection
- Silence detection (unexpectedly silent events)

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises
