## Overview

novaalert is an alarm and critical event management software developed by novalink, designed for reliable emergency notifications and responses across healthcare, industry, and public institutions.

## Features

- Automated Escalations
- Reporting And Analytics
- Two Way Communication
- Mobile Access
- Voice Alerts
- Audit Logs
- Push Notifications
- On Call Scheduling

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises
