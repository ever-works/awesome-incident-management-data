## Overview

DeskAlerts is a workplace communication solution for instant, effective emergency alerts and routine updates, reaching employees on any device, anywhere.

## Features

- Event Reminders
- Reporting And Analytics
- Desktop Alerts
- User Segmentation
- Corporate News
- Security Alerts
- Employee Training Notifications
- Real Time Notifications

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises
